Privacy policy

This app is used only by its owner, to access the owner’s own Gmail account.

  • Email data retrieved through the Gmail API is stored only on the owner’s personal computer.
  • It is not sent to, shared with, or sold to anyone else, and is not used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
